Privacy Policy — Instant Meme Generator

Last updated: October 2026

Instant Meme Generator (“the Extension”) is a free browser extension that lets you capture a region of your current browser tab, add a caption, and download the result as a meme image. This policy explains how the Extension handles your data. In short: it doesn’t collect any.

Data we collect

None. The Extension does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no external servers. There is no sign-up and nothing is sent to the developer or any third party.

How it works

When you click the Extension and choose to take a screenshot, it captures the visible area of your current browser tab and lets you select a region. The captured image and any caption you add are processed entirely on your own device, inside your browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. The finished image is saved only when you choose to download it, to a location you pick on your computer.

Permissions

The Extension requests only the minimum permissions needed to work:

  • activeTab / scripting — to capture the current tab and show the region-selection overlay, only when you click the button.
  • downloads — to save the finished meme to your device (if used).

These permissions are used solely to provide the feature described above and for no other purpose.

Children

The Extension is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children under the age of 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date.
